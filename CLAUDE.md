**Features**

- Accounts — register, log in, log out, persistent sessions
- Friends — send/accept/reject friend requests, friends list
- Recipes — create, edit, delete recipes with ingredients, steps, dietary tags, and public/friends/private visibility
- Personal pantry — ingredients with quantity, unit, expiry date, price paid
- Personal meal calendar — plan recipes onto dates, mark planned/completed, share with friends
- Feed — friends' shared recipes and planned meals
- Shopping list — auto-built from planned meals minus pantry, priced, checked against weekly budget
- Cook mode — step-by-step cooking with scaling and timers
- Settings — profile, diet, dislikes, budget, default visibility/servings, password
- "Who else is cooking this" — live presence card on a recipe page showing others currently cooking it
- Household — shared pantry, house calendar, members, create/join a household
- Shared shopping — claim/unclaim shopping-list items live, shared budget ticker
- Expiry race & pantry nudges — claim soon-to-expire items ("use tonight"), live nudges when a housemate's addition unlocks a meal for you
- Live cooking — friends' live cook sessions with step progress and reactions, "cook with Alice" companion mode
- Global live cooking — worldwide live cook sessions, filterable by pantry overlap/cuisine/diet, live help requests, translated Q&A threads
- Schedule — shared meal calendar with live presence ("Alice is looking at Thursday"), friend's recipe draft with live step-by-step comments
- Meal planner — budget/expiry/diet-aware meal suggestions and a draft week plan, AI-explained picks
